# Scientific source reading

## Setup

Use a full paper from a reference library or supplied document, a destination for analysis notes, and the bundled `references/reading-note.md` or an equivalent research template. Keep source material and generated notes outside the public checkout.

## Procedure

1. Resolve the exact paper and bibliographic identity. Distinguish preprint, accepted and published versions, and verify identifiers against authoritative metadata.
2. Inspect supplied annotations before interpretation. Include highlights, comments and embedded PDF annotation objects, enabling PDF extraction explicitly when the tool offers it. Use `pdf-annotations` for ink and visually inspect rendered pages. Record uncertain transcription and an unexpected mismatch between the supplied annotation count and the retrieved copy.
3. Identify the research genre and read the full available source, including relevant tables, figures, methods and appendices. An abstract or summary is not full reading. Record inaccessible material, extraction errors and omitted sections.
4. Reconstruct the paper's research question, argument, evidence, method and contribution on its own terms. Separate the authors' claims, supplied annotations and the analysis. Retain page/section locators for consequential claims and quotations.
5. Evaluate what the evidence supports, the inference's scope, alternatives and limitations. Do not invent tests, data, citations or a stronger claim than the paper supports.
6. Search relevant source notes and literature when requested. Read proposed connections before asserting them. Graph links and similarity scores identify candidates; they do not prove substantive agreement.
7. Check identifiers and existing note identity before creating a duplicate. Preserve prior source annotations and independently authored text. Create the analysis note with verified metadata, source coverage, argument, evidence/method, contribution and uncertainty.
8. Verify citations and quotations against the source and return the note with its reading/annotation coverage. Do not imply review of an unavailable appendix or unread source.

## Boundaries

This method analyzes scientific sources. It does not supply personal reading tiers, attention routines, editorial judgments, author voice profiles or private research commitments. Ordinary source reading does not require a local model. Explicit restrictions attached to a source remain binding; an unavailable optional model is not a reason to fabricate a reading receipt.
